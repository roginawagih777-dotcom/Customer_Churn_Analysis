# Customer Churn Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn in the telecommunications industry to identify the main factors that influence customer retention and cancellation. The goal is to help businesses understand customer behavior and develop effective retention strategies.

## Project Objectives

* Analyze customer churn rate and customer retention.
* Identify customer segments with high churn rates.
* Investigate the impact of contract type and payment method on churn.
* Identify customers who may be at risk of leaving.
* Provide actionable business recommendations to reduce churn.

## Tools & Technologies

* Python: Data cleaning and exploratory data analysis.
* Pandas: Data manipulation and preprocessing.
* Matplotlib: Data visualization.
* Power BI: Interactive dashboard and KPI visualization.
* GitHub: Project documentation and version control.

## Data Cleaning

The dataset was reviewed and prepared for analysis using Python.

Key preprocessing steps:

* Loaded the customer churn dataset using Pandas.
* Converted the TotalCharges column to a numeric data type.
* Handled invalid and missing values.
* Removed records with missing TotalCharges values.

## Dashboard KPIs

The Power BI dashboard includes the following key performance indicators:

* Total Customers: 7,032
* Total Churned Customers: 1,869
* Churn Rate: 26.58%
* At-Risk Customers: 1,138

## Key Insights

* The overall customer churn rate is 26.58%.
* Month-to-month contracts have the highest churn rate at approximately 42.71%.
* Customers with one-year contracts have a churn rate of approximately 11.28%.
* Customers with two-year contracts have a churn rate of approximately 2.85%.
* Electronic check users have a high churn rate of approximately 45.29%.
* Customers with shorter tenure and higher monthly charges are important segments to investigate for churn prevention.

## Business Recommendations

* Encourage customers to switch from month-to-month contracts to longer-term plans.
* Investigate the reasons behind the high churn rate among electronic check users.
* Offer targeted retention incentives to customers with short tenure and high monthly charges.
* Improve onboarding and customer support for new customers.
* Monitor churn KPIs regularly to evaluate retention strategies.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how data analysis and visualization can help businesses understand customer churn, identify high-risk customer segments, and make data-driven decisions to improve customer retention.
